Initial v1.0.0 release of VaultZero:
• Offline zero-knowledge AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) cryptographic vault
• Hardware-backed Android Keystore BiometricPrompt CryptoObject unlock
• Support for Logins (with live RFC 6238 TOTP), Payment Cards, Secure Notes, Identities, SSH Keys, and BIP-39 Seed Phrases
• Password, Passphrase, and Seed Phrase cryptographic generators
• Offline Security Audit Engine, Duress Vault, Panic Wipe, and encrypted .vzero backup/restore
